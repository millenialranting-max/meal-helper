Verdict The ads get attention: CTR, link CTR and CPM are all healthy. Revenue is lost after the click. Tracking misses most of the journey, nothing brings back warm visitors, a single pack sells for less than it costs to get a sale, and 1 ad took half the budget. Fix these before adding budget.

Spend went up +93% from W1 to W4 while purchases fell −20%. Cost per purchase rose from €29 → €70. Even the best weeks only broke even on ad spend, before product cost.

Funnel: where the drop happens

All campaigns, 28 Aug – 25 Sep · benchmarks from our Meta playbook
Top of funnel passes: CTR 2.45% (≥2% ✓), link CTR is 73% of CTR ✓. Most clicks never show up as a page view. Part of that is real (slow load, accidental taps). Part is tracking (see fix #1). Every step after the page view passes the benchmarks.

Top 3 fixes ranked by impact · estimates assume ~€40/day spend

1Fix the measurement

The browser pixel records only ~30% of what the server (CAPI) records (PageView 959 vs 3,000, Purchase 11 vs 36, last 27 days). The CookieYes banner probably blocks the pixel until visitors accept. Send Purchase via CAPI with hashed email, phone and external_id, and deduplicate with event_id.

est. −10–20% CPA · fills retargeting pools

2Turn on retargeting + refill

0% of budget goes to warm people today. That leaves ~125 abandoned carts a month, 3k+ IG/FB engagers and 70k+ video plays unused. Put 15–20% of budget on warm, hot and refill ad sets. Buyers of a 30-day pack come back around day 25–40.

est. +8–12 orders / month

3Sell the Combo, focus the account

A single pack is 2,549–2,799 RSD (≈€23), less than the €43.5 it costs to get a sale. Lead with Combo Day+Night 4,699 RSD. Run one always-on prospecting campaign and one test campaign, and stop relaunching.

est. AOV +25–30% · ROAS 0.71 → ~0.9+

  • Weekly check: fix the first funnel step that fails
