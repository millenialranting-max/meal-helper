Older buyers (55+): separate campaign or separate creative?

Separate creative, same campaigns
  • Too little data for a campaign. The signal is 5 purchases on €111. A 55+ campaign would split €40/day into pieces too small to leave learning.
  • Creative is the targeting now. With a broad audience, Meta shows each ad to the people who react to it. An ad with a 60-year-old in a slava kitchen finds 55+ buyers on its own.
  • What to do: the existing video creators are mostly young or fitness types, so fix the age gap in the statics. Make T9 (a 55–65 host at slava) and use 45–65 faces in T4, T6 and T8. Winners move into Campaign 1, where Meta keeps sending them to 55+.
Only if you want proof: in week 3–4, run a Meta A/B test (Experiments) with the same 3 statics: broad vs age 45–65+, €5/day each, 14 days. Build a separate 45+ campaign only if its CPA is ≥30% lower after 10+ purchases.

Launch day checklist

  • Create audiences: 50% video viewers 60d, site visitors 30d, buyers 25d / 30d / 45d. Engagers, cart 14d and buyers 180d already exist.
  • Reuse existing posts for W1, W2 and R1 (use the post ID) so they keep their likes and comments.
  • Download the 6 videos from the ad library for the re-edits. Kajra has never run as an ad.
  • Pause both current campaigns the day C1–C3 go live, so nothing bids against itself.
  • Name everything like T04_Day_Otkopcano_Static_v1 so results are easy to read.

Rules to run it by target CPA ≈ €25 (break-even on a Combo order at ~60% margin, confirm margin)

Test → Winners
  • After 1,000 impressions: CTR ≥ 2% and link CTR ≥ 1%
  • and cost per add-to-cart ≤ €10, or at least 1 sale
  • Move it in using the same post ID
Kill
  • €50 spent with 0 sales
  • CPA above €35 after €100 spent
  • Weekly frequency above 3 with falling CTR: refresh the hook
Scale
  • +20% on Winners (≈ +€5/day) every 3–4 days while CPA is on target
  • Never double the budget in one go
  • Keep Test at €10–15 and Retarget at ~15%
Retargeting
  • Judge Warm on cost per add-to-cart
  • Judge Hot and Refill on sales
  • If Refill has <200 people, pause it and let Hot run
