Strategy

1

Stock the best sellers before 20 Nov

Chocolate and Vanilla Caramel are over half of flavor sales. Vanilla Caramel sold 1 unit in June because it was out. Keep both sizes in stock with a reorder point, never advertise what's out, and fix the homepage line "6 ukusa".

2

Bring the Combos back as the main offer

2× and 3× packs were 24–37% cheaper per kg and brought 40–66% of revenue from Dec to Jul. Lead with 2×1kg and 2×500g. Keep 3× for stocking up before a fast.

3

Own "posni protein"

Fasting drives demand. Orders jump 3.5× in the first week of Lent and fall ~60% the week after Easter, in both years. Make it the headline on the site and in ads, and plan the year around Božićni post (28 Nov – 6 Jan) and Veliki post (15 Mar – 1 May 2027).

4

Fix trust and claims

Product pages show 0 reviews next to "10.000 zadovoljnih kupaca", so add real reviews. Have a lawyer check claims like "jača imunitet" and the diabetes and insulin-resistance lines.

5

Keep EasyGut out of Yea's budget

From Apr to Jun, 31% of the Yea account's spend went to EasyGut, which returned only 0.2–0.8× its cost. Give it its own budget so it never pulls money from Yea.

Ads

1

Fund the seasons, then scale on real revenue

Follow the calendar above. Raise budget in +20% steps while total revenue ÷ total ad spend stays ≥3×, which is the 2025 level (confirm the target against margin).

2

One always-on prospecting campaign

Advantage+ Sales, broad Serbia, showing the best sellers and the Combos. Sour Cherry runs as one ad, not the whole campaign, since it is only ~5% of demand.

3

Retargeting always on

Meta reports 2–15× return on just €2–3/day. Run €5/day, or €15/day in peaks, with three groups: cart 14 days, visitors 30 days, and a refill group for buyers from 25–45 days ago (1 kg ≈ a month of daily shakes).

4

A "posni" creative set live before 28 Nov

5–6 ads: a posni breakfast recipe, a shaker routine, a flavor lineup carousel, a Combo value static, and creator videos run as partnership ads. Don't use health-professional titles in ads.

5

Plan the offers around the fasts

"Spremi se za post" Combos from 20 Nov leading into Black Friday 27 Nov, and a Lent bundle from 8–15 Mar.

6

Check Google Search, measure in Woo/GA4

Paid search orders fell from 255 to 22 a month, so check the budget and run "posni protein" and brand searches in season. Judge results by weekly orders and revenue: Meta once counted 773 purchases for one campaign in a month when the whole site had 601 orders.
