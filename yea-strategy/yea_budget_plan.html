Fasts cost €7–10 an order. Off-season months with €4–7k budgets cost €14–18, because extra money off-season buys few extra orders. Under €2k off-season stays at €7–9.

How much per season

Peak (Lent): €4–5k a month, about €115–150 a day on Meta.
Fast (Božićni post, BF): €2.8–3.6k a month.
Off-season: €1.5–1.8k a month. More than ~€2k off-season wastes money.
Google: Brand €250 every month, +€350 "posni protein" search in Lent.

Weekly check: cost per order

Total ad spend that week ÷ all WooCommerce orders that week.
Under €10 → raise budget +20% (up to the "max" column).
€10–13 → hold, swap in fresh ads.
Over €13 for 7 days → cut 20%. Never above €15 (that is under 2.5× return).

Stock decides first

If Chocolate or Vanilla Caramel is out, run the off-season budget whatever the month says. Order tubs for the next two months before each fast (column "Tubs").
