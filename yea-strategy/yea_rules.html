Budget rules & landing pages

How much to spend and when, which ads to run, where they lead.

1

Spend follows the season

1× = your normal week. Set it from what stock and margin allow, then multiply. In the first week of Lent orders were 3.5× the weeks before, and they fell ~60% the week after Easter, in both years.

2

Stock decides how far you go

Green: go to the season level
Chocolate and Vanilla Caramel each have 6+ weeks of stock
Run the full season level from rule 1.
Yellow: hold at 1×
2–6 weeks of stock, or one size missing
Push the flavors and Combos you have; order more now.
Red: retargeting only
Under 2 weeks, or a top flavor sold out
Pause ads for missing flavors. Restock before you spend.

Weeks of stock = units in stock ÷ units sold in a normal week. In a fast, stock runs out about twice as fast, so halve it.

3

Scale or cut every week, on real revenue

Under 2×2 weeks in a row: cut 20%, check stock, offer and page
2–3×Hold the budget, swap in fresh ads
3× or moreAdd 20%, at most twice a week

× = shop revenue (WooCommerce) ÷ total ad spend, that week. 3× was the 2025 level; set your own line from margin. Don't use Meta's purchase count for this.

4

Split the money the same way, always

~80% new customers (one prospecting campaign)
~20% retargeting

Retargeting covers cart, site visitors and refill (500g lasts ~2 weeks, 1kg ~4 weeks). It never stops, even in Red. Test new ads off-season; in peaks, run the winners.
