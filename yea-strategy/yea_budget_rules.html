3. Rules for raising and lowering

1
When: Regular and Retargeting once a week (Monday, last 7 days). Special sales every 2 days, because they are short.
2
How much: +20% up or −20% down, one step per check. The only bigger jump is the planned switch on the first day of a period.
3
Raise only if all 3 are true: the type is in "Raise if", everything together is ≤ €10, and Chocolate + Vanilla Caramel are in stock.
4
Undo a raise if the next week brings less than 1 extra order per €12 of extra spend. Go back to the last budget that worked.
5
Special sales: max 14 days, max 40% of total spend, on top of Regular. Lead with best sellers or Combos. A sale on a flavor with ~5% of demand (Sour Cherry) stays at the low end (€15/day).
6
Stock first: if a best seller is out, Regular drops to €10/day and nothing is raised until it is back. Never advertise what is out.
