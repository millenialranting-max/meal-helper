How every landing page should look

  • Same promise as the ad. A Chocolate ad opens on Chocolate, a Combo ad on the Combo, a posni ad on "Posni protein".
  • Buy on the first screen. Price, flavor picker and add-to-cart visible on a phone without scrolling; the button stays pinned.
  • Only what's in stock. Hide sold-out flavors and drop "6 ukusa" while there are 3.
  • Show value per kg. Put the 2× Combo next to single packs with price per kg and the saving in dinars.
  • Real reviews near the top. Every product page shows 0 reviews today.
  • Allowed claims only. Say what's in it; skip immunity, diabetes and skin claims until a lawyer approves them.
  • Fast on mobile. The homepage takes ~1.4 s to start loading; check PageSpeed.
