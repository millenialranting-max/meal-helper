What “1×” means: a normal week

~65orders a week
~70–100units a week (more with Combos)
~€850a week on Meta

That was a normal off-season week in autumn 2025, with every flavor in stock. Today it's ~20 orders a week on ~€70, because the ads are nearly off.

Set 1× to the weekly budget your stock can carry for 6+ weeks, and rebuild toward last year's level in +20% steps. Every other level is a multiple of it.

Why spend more in fasts: last year Meta spend stayed ~flat through Lent, yet orders jumped 4× in week 1. The same money buys far more orders then.

Last year's orders vs the budget to plan, × a normal week

1Stock light: check before every phase

Green: Chocolate and Vanilla Caramel have 6+ normal weeks → run the planned level.
Yellow: 2–6 weeks or a size missing → hold at 1×, push what you have, reorder.
Red: under 2 weeks or a top flavor sold out → retargeting only.

Weeks of stock = units in stock ÷ units sold in a normal week.

2Every week: scale or cut

3× or more revenue ÷ ad spend → add 20% (max twice a week).
2–3× → hold, swap in fresh ads.
Under 2× two weeks running → cut 20%; check stock, offer, page.

Use WooCommerce revenue. 3× was the 2025 level; set your own line from margin.

3Always the same setup

~80% to one prospecting campaign, ~20% to retargeting (cart, visitors, refill). Retargeting never stops, even in Red.
Test new ads off-season, 4–6 at a time; in peaks, run only the winners.

Landing pages 3 pages, one job each. Every ad goes to the page that repeats its promise. Letters match page 2.
